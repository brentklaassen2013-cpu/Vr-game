# Crazy Office: Night Shift — Reborn 1.1 Final Gauntlet

Clean-slate continuation of Reborn 1.0. The old v0.x lobby/game architecture is not used.

## Final Gauntlet improvements
- Enemy attack pacing now uses readable windups and global hit grace so groups cannot unfairly chain-hit the player.
- Active last-second shield movement can trigger Perfect Blocks with stronger haptics, score and stagger.
- Pressure / Flanker / Thrower workers now have distinct health, speed and damage profiles.
- Bosses use three HP phases; later phases increase arena-hazard pressure and shorten telegraphs.
- Boss HP and phase are surfaced in the HUD.
- Office props now have integrity, can visibly break down, lose damage after breaking, and reward property-damage play.
- Out-of-bounds prop velocity/position is clamped for long-session stability.
- Incidents now change gameplay: Lights Out, Printer Panic, Coffee Rush, Fire Drill and Mandatory Meeting have distinct effects.
- Survival gives small recovery at milestones; Riot sustains higher chaos.
- Career levels now give small capped starting resilience/improvised bonuses instead of being cosmetic numbers only.
- Higher career levels unlock harder contracts with higher rewards.
- Career salary milestones unlock automatic bat finishes: Standard / Chrome / Midnight / Executive.
- Run rewards now include style bonus from Perfect Blocks and best combo.
- Career statistics persist: shifts, total KOs, best combo, best rank.
- Dynamic procedural audio intensity reacts to Shift / Tension / Mayhem / Boss state.
- Bat crack stages now have a visible deformation cue and restore correctly on replacement.
- Hazard count is capped and hub transitions dispose hazards/bat state to prevent session buildup.
- Rank-ups announce during play; damage briefly boosts scene-key intensity for readable feedback.

## Honest remaining work
Real headset tuning is still required for controller offsets, reach, comfort, haptic strength and actual physical feel. Production 2–4 player multiplayer also requires host-authoritative networking plus multi-headset testing. Professionally recorded voice/audio assets would be a separate production pass.
